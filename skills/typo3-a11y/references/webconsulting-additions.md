# webconsulting integration: typo3-a11y

Load this skill only for its stated task; importing it does not authorize its installers, remote changes, deployments, auto-merge, or credential access.
Project/user approval and this collection's safety rules remain authoritative.

## Composition boundary

`typo3-accessibility` owns local markup remediation and manual verification; `typo3-wcag22-aa-agentic`
owns a requested whole-site accessibility audit. Use this upstream skill as their on-demand pattern
reference, or when named explicitly. Within an upgrade, verify affected widgets and final axe evidence;
do not start another complete audit programme or page/state matrix.
